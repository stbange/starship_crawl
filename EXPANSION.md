The Reclaimer expansion turns exploration into an expedition aboard **HARROW**, a sanctuary ark carrying sleeping cities. ORISON, the ship's caretaker, has grown an infested nervous system through its decks. A dead captain's records point to a way to wake the beacon and extract the survivors.

Begin at the marked console near the loading bay lift. **G** uses a terminal, **I** opens the character and mission journal, **Z/X** or the mouse wheel changes weapons, and **1–9/0** upgrades the ten attributes. The browser menu exposes the same actions and unlocked weapons. Mobile adds **USE** and **GUN** beside the existing controls. The journal pauses play on the shared machine; Enter resumes. **L** cycles collected records; the browser menu also lets you select a record.

The six-stage mission route is:

| System | Target cell [across, deck, inward] | Objective |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Distress console | [0, 0, 0] | Accept the reclaimer's task in the safe bay. |
| Choir reactor | [1, 0, 0] | Clear nearby creatures and restore power. |
| Memory archive | [1, 1, 1] | Recover a human authorization key. |
| Command relay | [0, 1, 0] | Authorize access to ORISON's heart. |
| Neural heart | [2, 1, 2] | Defeat ORISON's Warden and sever the infestation's command link. |
| Beacon | [0, 0, 0] | Return, ignite the beacon, and extract the survivors. |

Terminals stand five metres beyond each cell's cyan lift pad. Target coordinates and the difference from your current cell appear in the journal. Regional paths can turn and loop; F3 shows connections. Use E/Q on lift pads to travel between decks. Each completed system grants two points, 75 salvage, restored health/shields/energy, and a checkpoint for participating players. Reactor, archive, relay, and heart completion unlock Plasma Lance, Reliquary Rail, Arc Conductor, and Storm Relic respectively. Extraction records a persistent victory. Exploration continues after victory; N then Enter starts another ship with your characters and equipment.

Three optional contracts award two points and 100 salvage each: slay 12 creatures, collect eight caches, and restore three systems. Each pays once per expedition. In split-screen the objectives and caches are shared, while defenses, weapons, stats, salvage, and collected records belong to each player. One cache goes to one player. Mission and contract rewards go to currently participating players.

Pools, lounge furniture, chandeliers, gaming machines, command consoles, workbenches, coolant banks, forge mouths, quarantine bars, medical beds, cryopods, planter beds, containers, and archive racks distinguish these spaces. The four-chunk halls remain enormous, and maintenance conduits still require crouching. Most lights are broken; the flashlight has no battery limit.

Distance gates stronger species and scales health. Armor reduces incoming weapon damage; frontal wards reduce it further except while charging an attack. Flank warded creatures or use splash and chaining. Brood spawning stays within the room's 20 encounter slots and respects persistent kill identities. Climbing uses actual collision surfaces. Closer creatures take priority within the bounded rendering budget.

Ten attributes each accept up to 50 upgrades. Spend one ascension point, or salvage when no points remain; salvage cost is 60 plus 25 per current attribute level. Upgrades refill defensive and weapon resources.

| Attribute | Effect per upgrade |
| --- | --- |
| Damage | +35% of base weapon damage |
| Vitality | +25 maximum health |
| Haste | +10% to the fire-rate multiplier |
| Armor | +12 armor capacity and increased damage absorption, capped at 65% |
| Shield | +15 shield capacity and +2 shield recharge per second |
| Mobility | +2.5% movement speed |
| Precision | Reduced spread and +2 percentage points of critical-hit chance |
| Recovery | +1.5 health regeneration per second |
| Reactor | +20 energy capacity and +2 energy regeneration per second |
| Salvage | +2 currency per kill and +5 per salvage cache |

Damage consumes the shield first, then armor absorbs part of the remainder and loses durability. Shield and health recharge after 2.5 seconds without damage; weapon energy recharges continuously. Medkits, shield cells, armor plates, energy cells, salvage, weapons, and ship records appear as colored caches near room lift routes. Full health/shield/armor/energy supplies remain available until useful. Collected caches and defeated encounter identities remain consumed after unloading and saving. Death retains progression and equipment and restores defenses at the checkpoint.

Generator version 3 migrates version-1 and version-2 characters into a fresh expedition. The current ship cannot be retained across that generator change. Save format 5 retains psionics and the fold shelter while preserving compatible format-3/version-4 expeditions; see [SHELTER.md](SHELTER.md). Unknown or corrupt files are rejected before changing the active session. Export a browser save for a portable backup.

The content uses procedural geometry and shared behavior systems. More authored landmarks, multi-deck architecture, navigation across complex room routes, randomized mission locations, equipment item inventories, and device performance tuning remain future work. Automated checks validate builds, core mechanics, browser controls, saves, topology, and rendering; long-session balance still benefits from hands-on play.
