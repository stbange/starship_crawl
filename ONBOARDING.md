# Learning an expedition

New ships begin with one pulse carbine, a flashlight and navigation. Explore the
ship, shoot attackers and use the bay console to accept the commission. The map,
objective, movement, interaction, recovery and accessibility settings are available
immediately. Nothing waits for a tutorial acknowledgement.

| Progress | What becomes available |
| --- | --- |
| Three kills, then a quiet moment | Breach shotgun; weapon switching and basic character upgrades |
| First elite or phase guardian defeated | Shared fold shelter for rest, medical care and cooking |
| First phase guardian defeated | Full attribute sheet; recoverable matrix interfaces in ordinary rooms from layer two |
| Interface recovered | A separate terminal interaction enters a protected first link; restore power and return to see the lights |
| First power restoration | Full matrix controls, Veil/Echo/Interrupt and normal network exploration |
| Second guardian tier | Reward choices include psionic powers |
| Third guardian tier onward | Reward choices include rigs and specializations |

An interface can be recovered when the terminal's immediate area has no nearby
visible threats. Dormant optional encounters and enemies behind walls do not
block it. The first link protects the body and avatar, offers one power-restoration
task and disables combat/program farming. Completing it returns to the physical
room. Later links use the regular matrix rules. Hacking remains optional; every
mandatory fight and route is achievable with ordinary guns and movement.

Near-bay ordinary encounters have at most three readable ground attackers, with
a limited ranged introduction in low rooms or on adjoining decks. The ship keeps
its varied architecture. The hidden director cannot add pressure in this opening
area or during a new-equipment practice opportunity. Early guardian and optional
challenge rewards respect the same power/vehicle tiers.

## Choose how much guidance to see

**F1 → Guidance**, **browser Menu → Controls and display → Guidance**, or the
**Quest Y menu → Guidance** cycles/selects:

- **Guided:** basic exploration and new-system lessons, one at a time after danger passes.
- **Minimal:** earned-equipment and new-system instructions, without basic lessons.
- **Off:** no tutorial or reward-practice hints. Objectives and combat feedback remain.

Use **Backspace** or **Dismiss current hint** to dismiss a lesson. **/** opens the
field guide; the browser and Quest menus also provide **Field guide**. Native
controller players can pause, press **Back/Select**, then choose Field guide or
Dismiss hint. Hints use the active keyboard, controller, touch or Quest bindings.
They complete through use or after twelve visible seconds. Feedback messages and
combat interrupt their display without silently marking an unseen lesson read.

Guidance is saved per local player in device preferences, separately from the
expedition. Importing a save or starting another ship preserves the preference.
Lesson receipts belong to each character and survive saves; a new character
starts new receipts. A co-op partner's receipts are independent. The shelter is
shared, while matrix interfaces are recovered individually.

## Compatibility

New ships use generator **15**, save format **16**, narrative **3**, hidden faction
simulation **1**, and matrix generator **1**. Generation retains the generator-14
architecture; only the new opening encounter rules and staged rewards change.
Earlier saved ships retain their geometry, rewards, equipment and matrix/shelter
access. Choose **N, Enter** or **Begin a new ship** to try the staged opening.
Preferences format 2 adds guidance; existing format-1 settings migrate with Guided
selected. Invalid introduction receipts reject the import without changing play.
