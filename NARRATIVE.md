Each new ship offers a generated **rescue**, **knowledge recovery**, **alien technology salvage**, or **containment** expedition. Its seed determines its name, original purpose, catastrophe, controlling entity, survivor agenda, last manifest and catastrophe origin. A pilgrim ark carries pilgrims, a liner passengers, a carrier prisoners, and a seed vault custodians. The briefing names the job before you enter.

Use **G** at the loading bay's gold-marked console to accept the commission. Follow its lead or explore another room. Bootprints, cable repairs, thawed tags, scrubbed labels and ration shrines can reveal an alternative investigation. Actual room entry discovers evidence; streaming chunks and browsing maps do not advance the story.

**I** opens the expedition journal: current goal, confirmed facts, field observations and unresolved leads. **L** cycles records, **U** tracks another known lead on both maps, and **F4** switches expedition/character pages. **F6** opens the ship dossier. **F7** opens the expedition debrief, career results and recent event history. Unknown facts remain labelled until a witness investigation or matching physical notice confirms them. **Enter** closes either page. Journals also work inside the shelter. Use **E/Q** at cyan lifts to change decks.

Investigate a refuge signal, witness archive or containment ledger with **G**, after clearing exposed enemies near it. Evidence identifies a target and optional operation. Once assigned, their locations remain fixed. Completed sites establish checkpoints and award each participating player 75 salvage and two points. Passive historical notices grant information without mission progress or rewards.

At the target, **G** opens decisions. **1/2/3** confirms the displayed consequence; **Enter** leaves undecided. The third option requires the optional operation or a brokered council agreement. Follow-on chapters require their own relay. You must remain at the cleared target to commit a choice, and that choice is final for this expedition.

Rescue, knowledge and salvage can have a **direct commission** or a **dependent target**. Direct commissions let you complete the main job without a compulsory cost to the refuge; optional preparation expands the recovery. Dependent targets require a decision about a system the survivors need.

| Commission and arc | First outcome | Second outcome | Preparation enables the third outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct rescue | Evacuate everyone at this refuge | Establish a refuge aboard | Restore the remote relay and rescue another group of sleepers too |
| Dependent rescue | Evacuate the awake; immobile sleepers remain | Fortify the refuge; everyone stays | Secure the evacuation relay to bring everyone home |
| Direct knowledge | Copy the independent chart | Transmit the chart; leave the original | Rebuild the damaged catalogue and recover additional lost routes |
| Dependent knowledge | Take the living archive; the refuge loses its navigator | Transmit a survey; leave the archive | Build a memory mirror to recover a complete copy |
| Direct salvage | Take a spare alien drive without disrupting ship power | Transmit its blueprints; leave the drive | Restart the fabricator and recover its technology too |
| Dependent salvage | Take the drive; the refuge loses power | Leave the drive powering life support | Repair the backup reactor and take the drive safely |
| Containment | Purge the source and destroy its research | Seal the live source aboard | Restore isolation to contain the source while preserving research |

Containment targets the physical catastrophe origin. Purging also permanently defeats its remaining room-owned infestation through the normal encounter records. Sealing and isolation preserve their stated research outcomes; power, beacon and controller operations also change the playable ship.

Return to the bay's extraction beacon to record one victory. The first outcome earns 250 salvage, the second 180, and the prepared outcome 400, in addition to ordinary site rewards. The debrief opens with the result and the journal retains the decision. Continue exploring, use **G** at the bay to accept another commission, or press **N**, then **Enter**, to generate another ship with the same character.

**History in the rooms.** Four catastrophe families link the physical ship and its accounts: ruptured cryonic regulators, a xenobiology outbreak, a defense-intelligence purge, or a reactor cascade. New generation favors related sectors and rooms. A real cryosleep gallery, lab, command room or reactor houses the origin and its damaged apparatus. Frost, bloom, quarantine or fire spreads through connected rooms with decreasing intensity; other regions may contain related blackouts or quarantine. Survivor waystations contain beds, a cooking station and notices. Controller stations carry lockout panels and notices. Entering these rooms after accepting the job records their accounts in the dossier, including during continued exploration after a decision. Facts and observed locations persist across saves.

Gamepads open the expedition journal with **left trigger + Back/Select**. **RB/right shoulder** cycles expedition, dossier and debrief pages. **X/west** reads the next record (or previous event in the debrief), **Y/north** changes the tracked lead, and **B/east** closes the journal. At a visible decision, **D-pad left/up/right** chooses 1/2/3. Browser touch menus provide both journals and labelled decision buttons, with the prepared choice disabled until its operation is complete. Journals pause the shared simulation in split-screen.

The first two milestones introduced **21 scene templates**: the original sixteen plus a containment source, isolation operation, and three passive historical records. Interactive destinations occupy the first four-by-four district across decks -1, 0 and +1, with architectural preferences and clear service positions. Evidence and historical notices can be discovered farther away. The completed system has **31 scene templates**. The ship remains endless, while each commission has a finite conclusion.

**Reactive operations and survivor groups.** Actual exploration reveals microgrid repairs and beacon sites; witness investigations reveal all four initial operations and the council. Recovering a controller cipher reveals a shutdown console. Once revealed, locations never move within the commission. Microgrids restore steady local point lights and add health, shield and energy recovery outside combat. A secured, powered beacon creates an 18-metre ward around its service station: hostiles stop targeting occupants and incoming damage cannot hurt them. Teal rings and map outlines mark powered wards; amber outlines mark unpowered beacons. Restoring power before discovering the council still powers its camp when it becomes known. Controller shutdowns silence room-owned turrets without deleting them or erasing enemies elsewhere. Systems persist in the ordinary room records across streaming and saving.

| Group | Priority | Assistance at 70 support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Refuge | Bring the ship's pilgrims, passengers, prisoners or custodians home | 100 additional salvage at extraction |
| Keepers | Preserve the ship and its living systems | Unlock the Lumen Cutter beam at extraction |

Their council offers evacuation priority, preservation priority, or a negotiated agreement. The agreement requires microgrid power and the controller cipher; both groups then help prepare the main target's third conclusion, providing an alternative to its original optional operation. Operations and conclusions change support, system state, transmissions and later commissions. Taking a dependent refuge's drive without the backup reactor leaves its camp dark, removes its powered ward and makes relief urgent. That situation can be repaired during a later refuge commission. Character progression and forgiving respawns continue normally.

**After extraction.** The debrief shows the chosen result; people rescued and still aboard; recovered knowledge and technology; contained threats; faction support; camp systems; career totals; and recent events with their original locations and outcome counts. **L** or gamepad **X** browses earlier events. The browser menu supplies debrief and event buttons. Rewards and career records update once per extracted chapter, for both participating players. Starting another ship preserves each character's career alongside their existing build and equipment.

At the bay, **G** accepts another commission on the same ship, including directly from the debrief. The next arc derives from the seed, earlier choices, recovered resources, survivor support and ship condition. Repeating the same save and actions produces the same offer and destinations. Each chapter has a witness/contact, optional relay, target decision and extraction at the bay. Most destinations extend deeper into the ship; HARROW returns to its original neural heart. Completed chapter locations and outcomes remain in the event history, while the next chapter reserves new destinations. System changes persist after those scenes finish. The event window retains the latest 32 events; cumulative outcomes retain all extracted results.

| Follow-on story | Target and immediate outcomes | Relay-assisted outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HARROW / ORISON's Heart | Sever ORISON to free captive crew, or negotiate to preserve its charts | Partition it to free the crew and preserve the charts |
| The Stranded Refuge | Evacuate the awake, or fortify their refuge aboard | Evacuate all remaining survivors |
| The Lost Catalogue | Recover its index, or transmit a survey | Reconstruct the complete catalogue |
| The Quarantine Wake | Purge the breach and lose its samples, or seal it aboard | Isolate it while preserving samples |
| The Dead Fabricator | Recover its core, or transmit its blueprints | Recover the core and its production library |

HARROW uses the existing Warden in the neural-heart room. Luring it away cannot bypass the target gate; killing it early remains a legitimate advantage and it does not respawn. Relays restore their own powered safe wards, and a refuge relay repairs a dark council camp. Follow-on preparation applies to its own chapter. Rescues draw from the ship's remaining recorded population, so repeated commissions cannot rescue the same people twice.

**Compatibility.** New ships use generator **6**, narrative **3**, and save format **9**. Format-8/narrative-2 expeditions retain their original history and story. Format-7/narrative-1 expeditions keep generator 4 and their original three-motivation story. Formats 3–6 retain their original ship and HARROW/ORISON campaign. Generators 3–6 reconstruct their original architecture unchanged, including after saving in format 9. Old expeditions start the reactive story only when the player requests a new ship. Formats 1–2 preserve profiles and announce a fresh expedition. Original collectible ship records remain in the character archive; expedition facts, events and outcomes live in the journals.
