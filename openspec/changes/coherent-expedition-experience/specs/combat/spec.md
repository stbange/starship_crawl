# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Complementary encounter roles
Coherent-expedition encounters SHALL combine compatible pressure, support and pursuit
roles within existing actor and effect budgets. Specialist commitments SHALL have
recognizable warnings, counterplay and recovery. Required encounters SHALL remain
solvable using equipment earned on the basic route without a specific optional
weapon, power, rig or matrix action.

#### Scenario: Break a shielded swarm
- **WHEN** a player disables the shielding specialist in a supported swarm
- **THEN** the shield connection visibly fails and the remaining creatures expose the promised opening without gaining replacement support from nowhere.

#### Scenario: Face a ceiling hunter
- **WHEN** a ceiling hunter commits while other creatures pressure the player
- **THEN** its warning remains identifiable, its movement follows actual surfaces and its committed attack permits a basic movement response.

#### Scenario: Compare earned builds
- **WHEN** starter, middle-phase and late-phase legal builds face their corresponding encounter fixtures
- **THEN** all retain a valid route to victory and specialists require their declared counterplay, without changing an engaged enemy's statistics to match the player's damage.
