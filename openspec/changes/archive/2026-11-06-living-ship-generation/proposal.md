## Why
Rooms still share predictable rectangular volumes and unrelated enemy populations. Milestone 3 should turn exploration into connected, inhabited places with dramatic changes of scale and readable local events.

## What Changes
- Generator 10 introduces elongated, bent and branching footprints, paired-deck vaults and upper galleries.
- Seeded environmental conditions link local tales, physical evidence, atmospheric effects and enemy ecology.
- Add bounded procedural decorative/atmospheric sprites, shallow water, colored rain, steam, motes and local fog.
- Preserve generators 3–9 and existing format-11 saves; new ships opt into version 10.

## Capabilities
### Modified Capabilities
- `ship-generation`: varied connected footprints, paired volumes and environmental ecology.
- `ship-history`: local environmental tales coherent with seeded damage.
- `presentation-input`: depth-tested retro sprites and per-view atmosphere.

## Impact
Core generation, streaming, collision, population and presentation; native/WebGL renderer; native, gameplay, coverage and packaged browser suites. No new dependencies or save-format reset.
