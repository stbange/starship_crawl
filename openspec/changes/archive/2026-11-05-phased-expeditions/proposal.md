## Why
The current sandbox grants advanced equipment immediately and retains power between ships. The approved direction is a fresh character per ship, earned capabilities, four progressively deeper phases, challenging guardians and seeded directional environments.

## What Changes
- Start both characters and matrix profiles fresh on every new ship; preserve checkpoint death, current-ship continuation and save/resume.
- Add four spatial phases, two guardian approaches per phase, an early optional elite, stable reward offers, individual receipts and shelter loadouts.
- Generate opposing thermal and order traits per ship, reflected in architecture, thermal dressing, encounter selection and map guidance.
- Add telegraphed guardian patterns, retry rules, meaningful equipment combinations and optional physical/matrix preparation.
- Integrate phase destinations with generated commissions and expose rewards, loadouts, routes and guardian status through desktop, gamepad and touch controls.
- Introduce generator 7 and save format 11. Preserve generator 3–6 expeditions and formats 1–10. Narrative 3 and matrix generator 1 remain compatible; new physical story placement is selected by ship generator.

## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `phased-expeditions`: fresh characters, phases, guardian attempts, rewards, loadouts and directional traits.
### Modified Capabilities
- `expedition`: new-ship reset policy.
- `progression`: ship-local ownership and equipped abilities.
- `persistence`: format 11 progression appendix and older expedition compatibility.
- `psionics`: require ownership and equipment in new expeditions.
- `equipment`: require the equipped earned rig in new expeditions.

## Impact
Core simulation, generation, collision, narrative, saves, rendering, input and browser menus. Validate strict current/archived OpenSpec, all native unit and gameplay suites, >=95% per-file/overall core line coverage, sanitizers, native rendering, Windows compilation and packaged browser interaction/persistence. Work remains on the requested feature branch.
