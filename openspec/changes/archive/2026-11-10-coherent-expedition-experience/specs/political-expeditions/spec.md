# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Sustained visible campaign consequences
Coherent-expedition campaigns SHALL connect supply, preparation, conflict and occupation or
retreat through persistent causes. Player interference SHALL affect the
corresponding resources and subsequent orders once. Physical evidence and
inhabitants SHALL communicate outcomes without exposing strategic controls.
Ignoring a campaign SHALL preserve required progression and continue its causal
simulation within existing budgets.

#### Scenario: Interrupt a siege supply line
- **WHEN** players destroy supplies bound for a known siege force
- **THEN** the receiving force's next eligible decisions reflect that loss and later observations show the resulting delay, altered strength or retreat without resetting casualties.

#### Scenario: Return after a factory changes hands
- **WHEN** a captured factory is revisited after streaming and save/resume
- **THEN** its occupants, supply activity and retained battlefield evidence match recorded events, while required entrances and story interactions remain usable.

### Requirement: Persistent director sequences
The coherent-expedition director SHALL connect clues, approach, pressure, confrontation and
quiet aftermath into bounded sequences. Progress SHALL follow actual observation,
actions and active simulation. Sequences SHALL adapt or end when players bypass
them. Both players' observations, confirmed outcomes, engaged enemy statistics and
known geography SHALL remain authoritative over proposed interventions.

#### Scenario: Defeat a threat before its planned confrontation
- **WHEN** players find and defeat the threat introduced by a sequence early
- **THEN** later beats reflect that victory instead of resurrecting it or spawning an equivalent replacement to force the planned fight.

#### Scenario: Leave a developing sequence
- **WHEN** players leave its area or enter recovery before a proposed pressure beat commits
- **THEN** the director can defer or cancel that unobserved beat, release reservations once and retain already observed clues and consequences.

#### Scenario: Pause a sequence in shelter
- **WHEN** every active player enters shelter or the game is suspended
- **THEN** sequence clocks pause with simulation and resume without a catch-up burst, while a partner remaining outside permits one shared simulation to continue.

#### Scenario: Read the normal interface
- **WHEN** players inspect maps, guidance or the journal during a sequence
- **THEN** discovered leads remain available without exposing sequence stages, director pressure scores, hidden relationships or army orders.
