# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Guardian systems and changing arenas
Coherent-expedition guardians SHALL expose actionable systems, warned stages and
arena changes caused by confirmed actions. Required traversal SHALL remain valid
without optional mobility tools. Optional physical or matrix preparation SHALL
produce a visible declared advantage; the unprepared fight SHALL remain winnable.
Victory SHALL open a visible route and persist its district consequence once.

#### Scenario: Destroy a coolant assembly
- **WHEN** participants destroy a guardian's coolant assembly
- **THEN** its associated attack changes or stops, the announced opening occurs and any moving geometry preserves a safe supported route for all participants.

#### Scenario: Prepare without the matrix
- **WHEN** a character who has not unlocked hacking operates an available physical sabotage route
- **THEN** the prepared advantage is available without granting matrix access or bypassing the required guardian victory.

#### Scenario: Retry an interrupted fight
- **WHEN** all participants retreat, die or resume a checkpoint captured during an attempt
- **THEN** attempt-local systems reset under the existing staging rules while completed preparation, earned rewards and previously defeated guardians remain durable.

#### Scenario: Exploit a strong build
- **WHEN** an earned combination destroys a guardian system quickly
- **THEN** the fight accepts that damage and advances according to its declared stages without adding hidden health or an arbitrary damage cap.
