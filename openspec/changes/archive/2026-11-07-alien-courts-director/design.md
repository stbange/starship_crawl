## Design
Three government templates (Divided Crown, Molting Court, Echo Parliament) are
permuted by ship seed. Infrastructure catchments span macro regions and decks,
with wilderness gaps. Each country has a reserved audience, disputed conduit and
royal-guard site; querying plans/maps never records discovery. Roles use clear
existing encounter aisles, maintain portals and preserve old generators.

An audience records a shared expedition pledge: restore supply, install a
successor or annex a disputed region. Existing optional squad combat supplies
conduit and finale challenges with ordinary-gun solutions. Persistent kills and
participation govern once-only equipment claims. A completed finale changes
relationships, rulers, territory or physical systems and grants bounded bonuses.
Per-player offense affects patrol hostility; neutral marked patrols defend their
room and do not attack a peaceful player. Courts offer repair after settlement.

The director owns no world RNG and never rewrites geometry or enemy statistics.
At most one dispatch is active: supplies, courier, architectural survey or an
invitation to an existing optional fight. Preconditions include actual physical
player presence, room readiness, pressure, both players' resources, cooldown,
budget, recent kind and previous dispatch room. Physical seals expire after three
minutes of active ship time. Claimed rewards, remaining time, sequence and bounded
reason history are serialized. Shelter/matrix-only sessions freeze this clock.

Format 14 appends explicit integer words after the unchanged format-13 payload.
Load validates bounded state before replacing the game. Legacy worlds write an
empty political appendix. Transient open audiences are reconstructed by USE.

## Scope and limits
This is three major powers and one finite dispute per power per ship. Dynamic
outcomes are deliberate local annexations, not an unbounded offline war simulation.
Political finales supplement the four existing phase guardians. Director conflict
events invite real existing battles; they do not spawn enemies behind the player.
