## Why
Exploration needs societies with understandable conflicts and lasting player
consequences, alongside bounded pacing that alternates pressure and discovery.

## What Changes
- Generate three multi-sector countries with alien governments, borders and patrols.
- Add audiences, shared-resource disputes, earned battle rewards and three outcomes.
- Add a bounded, saved encounter director with physical dispatches and decision logs.
- Resolve disputes through royal-guard finales, succession, treaties and annexation.
- Introduce generator 13 and appended save format 14; retain generators 3–12 and
  supported older saves without adding politics to existing expeditions.

## Impact
World generation, combat, navigation, rendering, input, saves and web publishing.
Validate strict specifications, native unit and gameplay tests, >=95% unit-only
coverage overall/per core file, packaged browser tests, render smoke and portable builds.
