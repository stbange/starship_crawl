## Why

Room themes currently repeat the same furniture clusters. Exploration needs more
spatial variety, readable evidence of the ship's history, and memorable discoveries.

## What Changes

- Generator 4 introduces four ship profiles, three interior floor-plan families,
  functional sector furnishings, connected incidents and four rare hall landmarks.
- Side spaces hold weapon and record caches; the main passage, lift and mission
  console remain accessible. Maps identify landmark discoveries.
- Generator 3 stays supported for saved expeditions. New ships use generator 4.
- Add deterministic, traversal, compatibility, coverage and browser checks.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `ship-generation`: varied interiors, connected history and rare discoveries.
- `persistence`: explicit generator-3 and generator-4 compatibility.

## Impact

Core generation, geometry, population, navigation, diagnostics and HUD; native and
web tests and documentation. Save format 6 stays unchanged. Open vertical shafts,
multi-deck geometry and interactive power/pressure systems remain future work.
