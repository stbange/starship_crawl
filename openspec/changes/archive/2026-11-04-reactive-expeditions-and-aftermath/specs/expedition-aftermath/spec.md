## Purpose
Finite story victories leave lasting results and lead into further commissions aboard an endless ship.

## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Lasting outcomes and debrief
Each extraction SHALL record its outcome once, award participating characters, and preserve rescued people, knowledge, technology and contained threats in expedition and career records. A debrief SHALL show consequences, faction responses, system state and recent events through keyboard, gamepad and touch controls.

#### Scenario: Revisit extraction
- **WHEN** players revisit a completed extraction or reload its save
- **THEN** the recorded outcome remains and rewards and career totals do not repeat.

### Requirement: Continuing commissions
After extraction players SHALL be able to explore, start a new ship with persistent characters, or accept another commission at the bay. Follow-on kind SHALL derive from seed and meaningful prior events; destinations SHALL remain fixed once revealed. Finite chapters SHALL support repeated follow-ons with bounded event storage.

#### Scenario: Continue aboard
- **WHEN** players accept a new commission after extraction
- **THEN** a playable investigation, optional preparation, target decision and return become available on the same ship with past effects intact.

### Requirement: HARROW and ORISON
HARROW/ORISON SHALL be one generated follow-on arc as well as a retained legacy campaign. Its neural-heart conclusion SHALL remain blocked while the original room-owned Warden lives, including when lured away.

#### Scenario: Defeat the controller guardian
- **WHEN** players reach the HARROW target while its Warden is alive
- **THEN** the conclusion is refused until they defeat it through ordinary combat.
