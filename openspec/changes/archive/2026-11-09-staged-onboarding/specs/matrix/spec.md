## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Separate linked player spaces
Players with matrix access SHALL enter a first-person network at physical terminals and a shelter workstation while retaining their body and return pose. Each player SHALL be able to explore independently in split-screen. Matrix and physical attacks SHALL remain isolated except for explicit system operations and body-danger ejection. New expeditions SHALL protect the body and avatar during their first power-restoration introduction; subsequent links SHALL use ordinary exposure rules.
#### Scenario: Guard a diver
- **WHEN** one player enters a field terminal after the introductory link and the other remains aboard
- **THEN** the avatar explores separately, the physical partner can guard the body, and body damage disconnects the diver.
#### Scenario: Learn the first operation
- **WHEN** a character enters their first introductory link
- **THEN** they can restore power safely and return to see its physical effect before ordinary matrix combat begins.

### Requirement: Solvable hacking encounters
After the introductory power restoration, players SHALL have a visible pulse, integrity, bandwidth and trace. Guards SHALL telegraph finite swept shots. Credentials, a routing pair and explicit force access SHALL keep controller routes achievable alone. Failure SHALL preserve confirmed work and character progress while ejecting with a short terminal lock. The protected introduction SHALL disable combat and program farming.
#### Scenario: Reach a protected controller
- **WHEN** a solo player lacks an advanced program
- **THEN** credentials, ordinary movement and switches or a declared force-access cost still provide a route.

### Requirement: Equipped programs
The matrix SHALL offer Veil, Echo, Interrupt, Bridge, Fork and Siphon, two equipped slots and persistent upgrades. Programs SHALL respect ownership, costs, caps and cooldowns, and unsuccessful activation SHALL NOT charge resources. New characters SHALL gain the initial program choices after their first power-restoration introduction; earlier expeditions SHALL retain their initial programs.
#### Scenario: Unlock an alternative
- **WHEN** a player finds a program cache and equips its program
- **THEN** its described stealth, distraction, interruption, bridge, routing or siphon effect becomes available and remains unlocked through saves and checkpoint death on this ship; a new ship resets the character.
