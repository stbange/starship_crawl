# Staged expedition onboarding

## Why

New players face too many controls and systems before learning exploration and combat.

## What Changes

Start new expeditions with a carbine and flashlight, introduce a second weapon through
combat, award shelter access at the first elite/guardian victory, and recover a matrix
interface from a terminal clear of nearby visible threats in the second band. Space guardian reward tiers so
powers precede vehicles and specializations. Reveal controls as they become useful.

Add Guided / Minimal / Off device preferences, contextual dismissible instruction and
a reviewable field guide. Preserve map/objective and accessibility controls in all modes.
Constrain near-bay encounters and protect first discoveries from director pressure.

## Impact

Generator 15 retains generator-14 architecture and adds bounded opening encounters.
Save 16 appends per-character instruction receipts and matrix introduction progress.
Existing ships retain their generator, equipment, access and reward tables. Preferences
migrate independently of expedition saves. Solo and optional co-op use individual lessons.

Validate specifications, native unit and gameplay tests, >=95% unit line coverage overall
and per core file, packaged browser controls and progression, rendering and separate Quest
build/tests. Physical Quest and novice human enjoyment remain manual checks.
