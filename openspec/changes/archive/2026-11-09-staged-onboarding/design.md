# Design

Gameplay progression is independent of guidance preferences. A new character earns the
shotgun after three kills; the first elite or phase guardian secures the shared fold shelter.
After opening phase two, an ordinary room with a clear terminal area in its band offers a recoverable matrix
interface. Taking it and logging in are separate interactions. The first power restoration
teaches a visible physical consequence before the full program inventory opens.

Early guardian choices offer weapons, the second phase adds powers, and the third adds
rigs/specializations. Existing reward drills remain useful and respect instruction settings.
New attributes appear after the first combat milestone; the initial choices emphasize the
three basic stats, with the full character sheet opening after the first phase victory.

One bounded lesson is presented at a time, deferred during danger. Actual use completes
lessons; dismissals and completed introductions survive saves. Settings survive new ships.
The map and story destination remain separate from tutorial instructions. Device-specific
prompts cover desktop, controller, touch and Quest. Menus only expose acquired systems.

Generation retains seeded architecture and limits nearby ordinary enemies to readable
ground attackers and a later ranged threat. Guardian rooms keep their earned challenges.
The director cannot add pressure in the opening catchment or during an equipment drill.
No mandatory route requires matrix, psionics or a vehicle. Bay recovery remains available.
