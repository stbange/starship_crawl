# Design

Extend the existing four-slot optional encounter grammar with elevated aeries,
clustered colonies, suppression batteries, courier circuits and matrix-supported
locks. Every fight remains defeatable using the starter gun. Specialized tools
provide spatial, resource or disruption advantages; they never become mandatory
keys. Fixed identities preserve kills, co-op participation and one-time claims.

Beyond layer four, contracts inherit heat, cold, order, chaos or alien identity.
Warned local hazards add tactical pressure. Victories evolve five bounded relic
effects at layers 5, 8 and 11 rather than inflate ordinary enemy health. Relics
modify actual shooting, mobility, shields and hacking advantages. Existing
campaign rewards remain in addition to these deeper milestones.

Nine district focal structures, repeated distant lights, framed approaches and
landing sightlines strengthen scale. Geometry stays outside essential routes and
within the existing chunk budgets. Express lift travel is a separate optional
proposal and is not required for these architectural changes.

Ten additional stable species implement listening, observation, reversal,
linked colony separation, one-time molting, wrong-foot machinery, recess
ambushes, distorted reflections, brittle cold hunters and pressure-fed ambushers.
Shared simulation states drive warnings, sound and animation. Observation uses
both players' unobstructed views. Colony pieces share health/identity/reward.
Wall runners transition onto actual ceilings; all attacks retain warning,
commitment and recovery and respect physical collision.

Verification retains the same unit coverage and gameplay assertions. The four-profile
ASan journey has a 1,200-second wall-clock budget (native remains 600 seconds),
because the two-profile instrumented journey measured 448 seconds and the former
four-profile 600-second budget expired under instrumentation. Simulation frame
and death bounds are unchanged. Browser failure logs are retained and annotated
in Actions, including the phase-regression step whose previous failure details
were hidden behind authenticated logs.
