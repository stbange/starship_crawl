## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Tool-rewarding encounters
Optional aeries, colonies, suppression batteries, courier circuits and cipher
locks SHALL reward flight, area attacks, mechanized armor, fast vehicles and
matrix services respectively. All SHALL remain escapable and defeatable with
ordinary weapons, with persistent kills and exactly one claim per participant.

#### Scenario: Choose a different build
- **WHEN** a player enters an optional encounter without its suggested capability
- **THEN** clear routes and a conventional combat solution remain available.

### Requirement: Architectural reveals
Generator 12 SHALL give major rooms district-specific focal silhouettes, framed
approaches, distant scale lights and landing invitations while retaining clear
portals, terminals, supported floors and bounded geometry.

#### Scenario: Arrive in a major volume
- **WHEN** a player enters through its approach or lift landing
- **THEN** a visible focal structure and lit direction invite exploration of its scale.

### Requirement: Directional deep contracts
Beyond layer four, optional contracts SHALL offer explicit regional risks and
ship-local relic evolutions at progressively deeper layers. Heat, cold, order,
chaos and alien contracts SHALL have distinct warned hazards and bounded
mechanical rewards without increasing the ordinary enemy health cap.

#### Scenario: Claim and revisit
- **WHEN** a participant wins, claims, saves and revisits a contract
- **THEN** the earned evolution persists without a second claim, and another
evolution requires a qualifying deeper layer.

### Requirement: Readable alien specialists
Ten additional species SHALL express distinct perception, movement or combat
behavior through matching animation and audible warnings. Observation SHALL
account for either player's unobstructed view. Attacks SHALL retain finite
commitments and recovery.

#### Scenario: Observe or evade
- **WHEN** a player watches a scavenger or sidesteps a locked attack
- **THEN** the scavenger stops or the attack misses without instant retargeting.

### Requirement: Physical alien anatomy
Colony fragments SHALL share one death and reward. Molting SHALL occur at most
once per living spawn. Ambushers SHALL occupy real recesses without sealing
exits. Reflections SHALL remain visual clues to a reachable body. Seam-runners
SHALL attach only to real walls and ceilings.

#### Scenario: Defeat a divided organism
- **WHEN** a colony fragment is shot or a molting sentinel is defeated
- **THEN** its shared organism awards only one death and reward.

### Requirement: Compatibility and verification
Generators 3–11 SHALL retain their generated architecture and populations.
Format 13 SHALL preserve both players' relics, read previous supported saves,
reject malformed progression transactionally and reset progression on new ships.

#### Scenario: Resume an older ship
- **WHEN** a format-12 generator-11 save resumes
- **THEN** its geometry, population, claims and character remain unchanged.
