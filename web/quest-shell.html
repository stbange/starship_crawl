Regular web game

Move with the left stick; snap-turn with the right. Right trigger fires; right grip interacts. A jumps or rides a lift up; B crouches or rides down. X switches weapons; Y opens the menu. Left grip shows the wrist map; left trigger toggles the flashlight. Click the right stick to recenter. Seated and standing play use the same calibration.

Use the sticks to travel. Leaning is supported near your seated or standing position. The view darkens if you lean through a wall or too far from that position. After tracking loss, release the controls and press A to resume.

Quest saves and settings are separate from the regular web game. Save import replaces this Quest expedition only. Rendering scale applies when entering VR. This initial VR build is single-player; headset performance and comfort still need physical Quest 3 testing.
