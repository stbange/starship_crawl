Every reclaimer starts with two fold rigs, three beam weapons and a Lantern Flare Cannon. Existing characters receive the four new personal guns when migrated. The original 16 weapon IDs and generator-3 cache rewards remain stable.

Rigs need clear supported ship flooring. They cannot deploy in space or inside the shelter, and occupied rigs cannot fold. Each player has their own mech and wagon. G boards the nearest owned rig within 3.6 metres and dismounts onto a clear supported position with an unobstructed exit route. Parked rigs block player movement. A blocked exit asks you to move to wider flooring. Rigs stay grounded; psionics are disabled while riding. Narrow ducts and low ceilings can require leaving them behind. Press their deployment key while on foot to pack them, then deploy again on suitable floor.

The mech changes the display to an amber pilot link, cockpit arms, plating meter and speed readout. Its base plating is **600**, plus 60 per armor upgrade, compared with the wagon's **150**, plus 15 per armor upgrade. Incoming hits consume personal shields, rig plating, then pilot armor and health. Depleted plating ejects the pilot; packing does not refill it. Your shelter's medical station repairs both owned rigs. Moving the mech normally reaches 5.5 m/s, or 8 while sprinting. The wagon accelerates toward 20 m/s, with gradual braking, and uses your personal weapon. Mobility upgrades also affect these speeds.

The mech's separate arsenal cycles through **Reaver Autocannon**, **Siege Rocket** and **Reaper Beam**. Its weapons use the pilot's rechargeable energy and damage progression. The autocannon fires explosive shells; rockets trade cadence for a large blast; the beam chills targets. Personal weapon ownership and selection are retained when dismounting.

The personal beam guns are **Lumen Cutter** (rapid burning ray), **Prism Lance** (long-range beam piercing up to four targets), and **Siphon Ray** (life-stealing beam). Their visible traces resolve on firing, stop at walls, and cost rechargeable energy. Holding fire sustains repeated beam pulses. The original 16 guns keep their travelling projectiles and impact effects.

**Lantern flares** travel visibly, pass through enemies without dealing damage, and attach to walls, floors, ceilings and the solid outer hull. Their warm point lights stay where they land. Base burn time is **30 seconds**, radius **12 metres**, and strength **2.5**. Each lifetime upgrade adds **15 seconds**; each strength upgrade adds **2 metres** of radius and **0.75** strength. Both tracks have five levels, reaching 105 seconds, 22 metres and 6.25 strength. Each purchase uses one ascension point, or otherwise 80 + 50 × current track level salvage. Upgrades affect future flares. At 32 attached flares, a new one replaces the one closest to expiring. Pause stops their timers.

Save format **6** retains rig locations, facing, plating, deployment and mounted state, mech weapon selection, flare upgrades, attached lights and their remaining burn time. Beams and travelling shots clear on load. Compatible format-3/4/5 expeditions keep their seed, missions, changed-room records, sanctuary and learned powers. New expeditions keep character upgrades and weapon ownership, refill packed rigs, and clear old deployments and lights. Generator **3** is unchanged.
