# Hidden factions: human playtest checkpoints

Use a new generator-14 ship or import the earned checkpoints linked from the
[review](../reviews/2026-11-08-hidden-simulation/README.md). Export your own save
first. Record seed, build commit, platform, GPU, controls and whether this is solo
or split-screen. Automated Chromium touch and virtual controllers do not count
as physical-device sessions.

| Check | Action | Observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutrality | Approach a marked patrol without firing; investigate a nearby story terminal | Does its posture/sound make neutrality understandable? Does it leave access clear? |
| Warning | Graze a guard once, stop firing, then leave | Can you recognise the warning and eventual disengagement without reading a reputation meter? |
| Deliberate aggression | Attack repeatedly; try silencing the local relay before and after a report | Do retaliation and escape feel fair? Does the partner remain neutral? |
| A passing column | Follow a supply carrier through doors and a deck connection | Can you distinguish its destination from an attack on you? Do troops obstruct narrow routes? |
| Rival battle | Watch without firing, interfere, or bypass | Are the two sides identifiable in darkness? Is it rewarding to choose when to intervene? |
| Persistent aftermath | Leave a damaged squad or occupied station; travel away, save/reload and return | Do surviving troops, debris, lighting and occupation tell a coherent small story? |
| Recovery | Continue after a hard fight; visit shelter, then return | Is there time to recover? Do discovery and quiet periods feel natural? |
| Separate views | Join with F2 and explore different rooms/decks; shelter or enter matrix in one view | Does each player have readable threats, sound and a fair route? Does either lose observed content? |
| Guidance | Open M, F6 and the ordinary journal; use touch and controller equivalents | Are normal story/guardian goals clear, with no court/director controls on a new ship? |
| Long play | Continue at least 30 minutes after the fourth guardian | Does growing power leave meaningful choices? Record dull stretches and surprising encounters. |

Repeat neutrality, a moving column, a battle and split-screen on a physical
Windows machine/gamepad and on a mobile WebGL device. Check Safari separately.
Listen with headphones and speakers at normal volume. Report frame-rate dips,
unreadable warnings and sounds that become tiring; software rendering cannot
answer those questions.
